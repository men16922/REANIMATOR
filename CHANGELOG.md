# Changelog

## Unreleased

- The Cryo Bulwark's frost shield is now a model that lowers when it attacks. Stage 3 gains a coolant pipe wall and cryo racks.
- Each stage remembers your best clear time, fewest deaths and most kills. The clear screen shows NEW BEST and the stage select shows your best time.

- New Stage 3 foe: the Cryo Bulwark carries a frost shield that blocks Pulse and Scattergun shots from the front. Hit it from behind, while it attacks, or with Rail, Grenade, Flamer or Rocket.
- Each stage has its own sound: the Foundry Line plays lower and heavier, the Reactor Spire colder and brighter, with its own ambience, footsteps and an arrival sting.
- The Slag Foreman no longer turns into a flat white shape while it winds up; its counter window now pulses orange over the iron.
- Boss intro letterbox bars cover the full width again (they showed as two black squares in the corner).
- The Slag Foreman has its own model, voice, hit sparks and a name card when you first meet it.
- The Stage 3 boss is named the Spire Warden on its intro card, boss bar and objective.
- Nearby steam vent bursts shake the camera.
- The busiest combat scenes draw fewer objects (lighter shadows, simpler ammo and health pickups).

## 1.1.0

- The Trailer reel now shows all three stages (90 seconds).
- Three stages: the original facility plus Stage 2 Foundry Line and Stage 3 Reactor Spire, each with lever doors and an exit teleporter.
- Stages menu on the title screen. Stages 2 and 3 unlock when the previous stage is cleared.
- Stage title card on entry and a stage clear screen with time, kills, completion, and best time. Next stage, Stage select and Title buttons.
- Cleared stages and best times are saved in the browser. Older saves keep working.
- Debug hooks are no longer reachable from the URL in the released build.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Six connected sectors: Cryo Airlock, Central Transit Shaft, Sunken Mines, Bio-Labs, Upper Security Deck, Core Vault.
- Metroid-style abilities: Thruster Boots (double jump), Blast Core, Scan Lens, Thermal Plating, plus Energy Tanks.
- Six weapons, dash with brief invulnerability, counter, and the three-phase Core Warden boss.
- Meltdown escape sequence and three endings based on how much you collected.
- Minimal HUD with a short, skippable tutorial that can be replayed from Extras or the pause menu.
- Data logs appear as brief notices and are kept in an archive.
- Retro-styled interface, Classic View (320x200 dithered look), procedural music.
- Settings: volume, music and effects sliders, reduce flashing, gore level, difficulty (Story, Normal, Hard), assist options.
- Installable web app with fullscreen landscape manifest.
