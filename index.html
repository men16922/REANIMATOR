How it was revived

Only the player's core 2D movement physics were reconstructed from the 1995 DOS shareware Abuse (v1.05). The Full 3D Quarter-View Twin-Stick game layer, rigged character models, weapons, bosses, levels, lighting rules, and audio are all original new-design works.

  • Verified DOS Restoration (src/engine/motion.ts): Analyzed ABUSE.EXE (DOS/4GW LE -> ELF) with REA + Ghidra along cop_mover 0x14760 → mover 0x3a1d8 → 0x38704 → try_move 0x37e82 → 0x558e5. Proven byte-for-byte in the Restoration Lab (zero original DOS art/audio/map bytes ship).
  • New-Design 60 Hz Full 3D Simulation (src/engine/motion3d.ts, src/game3d/): Capsule 3D movement (`WASD`), walkable catwalk ramps, Jet-Dash i-frames, 4-weapon twin-stick arsenal, chain-detonating bio-barrels, jumpable boss shockwaves, and strict R3.3 visual-only headlamp isolation.
  • Rigged Multi-Clip Meshy 3D Layer (src/render3d/): Skeletal Mixamo-rigged Meshy `.glb` bipeds (operative with RightHand-socketed pulse-rifle, warden, bio-stalker) animated by Three.js AnimationMixer + SkeletonUtils.clone, plus PCFSoftShadowMap & selective UnrealBloomPass.
